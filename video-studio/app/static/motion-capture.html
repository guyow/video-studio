🕺 Motion Capture

Take the motion of a video — or yourself on webcam — and put it on someone else. Two routes: a 3D avatar (free, local, tracks body + hands + lips live) or a real person from a photo (AI, paid) who delivers the exact same performance with the original audio.

Mode

Upload footage of a person — the avatar copies their moves and lips, stuck right on the video.
The desktop mocap studio: webcam drives a VRM character live — face, eyes, mouth, fingers, whole body — and exports BVH/VRMA motion for Blender or Unreal. Same avatars work in both.

Skip the 3D avatar: a photo of a real person performs the source video — body, lips, timing, original audio kept. Paid (fal.ai), renders in the cloud, lands in Finished takes.
Pick the face. This is who you become: upload a sharp, front-facing photo (an AI-generated spokesperson, anyone you have rights to). Then Start camera, hit Record, and perform — the face delivers your exact performance with your voice. Free, rendered on this PC.
