## Script

I'm the founder of Liitt. And yes, I'm a fairy. Long story.

I made Fairy Flame for that weird little hour when your day is over…
but your brain absolutely did not get the memo.

One tiny flame-shaped gummy. No jolt. No knockout.
Just a softer mood, a little more ease, and maybe some actual fun.

Made in the USA, third-party tested, and no mysterious fairy dust.
I have standards.

Consider it a tiny exit door out of your own head.

Come try one. I'll leave the light on.
