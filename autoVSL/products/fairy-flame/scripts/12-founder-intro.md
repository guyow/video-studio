## SCRIPT

**[HOOK — founder reveal]**
I'm the founder of Liitt. And yes, I'm a fairy. Long story.

**[SETUP — the weird hour]**
I made Fairy Flame for that weird little hour when your day is over… but your brain absolutely did not get the memo.

**[PRODUCT — tiny, no jolt]**
One tiny flame-shaped gummy. No jolt. No knockout. Just a softer mood, a little more ease, and maybe some actual fun.

Made in the USA, third-party tested, and no mysterious fairy dust. I have standards.

**[PAYOFF — exit door]**
Consider it a tiny exit door out of your own head.

**[CTA — soft, warm]**
Come try one. I'll leave the light on.
